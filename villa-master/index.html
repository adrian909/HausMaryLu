Haus MaryLu – Apartments Knetzersdorf (near Melk, Wachau)

The ideal place for relaxation and comfort in a dream setting!

A beautiful place to enjoy life.



Haus MaryLu is located in Knetzersdorf, 15 km from Melk Abbey, and offers river views, free WiFi, and free private parking.

Each unit features a terrace with city views, a flat-screen TV with satellite channels, a fully equipped kitchen, and a private bathroom with a shower and a hairdryer. Guests also have access to a minibar, a stovetop, a toaster, as well as a coffee machine and an electric kettle.

Guests can relax in the property's garden.

This apartment is 29 km from Lilienfeld Abbey and 31 km from the Erzherzog Franz Ferdinand Museum. Vienna International Airport is 99 km away.
